Our Instructors

Meet Yonna

Yonna is an experienced yoga instructor with over 10 years of experience. She is passionate about helping new students feel comfortable and supported in their yoga journey. Her teaching focuses on clear instructions, safe movements and creating a welcoming environment for all practitioners.

Meet John

John teaches gentle and restorative yoga, focusing on helping students find balance and relaxation through mindful movement. He encourages students to move at their own pace and provides alternatives for different levels of mobility and experience.

Accessibility Accommodations

We welcome students with different abilities and mobility needs. Instructors can provide seated or modified versinos of many poses, and students may use supportive equipment when needed.

Studio Policies

  • Arrive 10-15 minutes early to class to settle in and prepare.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows for movement.
  • Bring your own yoga mat, or rent one from the studio.
  • Inform the instructor of any injuries or health concerns before class.
  • Respect the space and other students by keeping noise to a minimum.

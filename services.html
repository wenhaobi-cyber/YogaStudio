Yoga Class Types

Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga is a slower-paced class that focuses on stretching and relaxation. It is suitable for beginners and those looking for a more restorative practice.

Vinyasa Flow

Vinyasa Flow is a dynamic class that links movement with breath. It is suitable for those with some yoga experience and looking for a more challenging practice.

Restorative Yoga

Restorative Yoga is a deeply relaxing class that uses props to support the body in various poses. It is suitable for all levels and is especially beneficial for stress relief and relaxation.

Private Sessions

We offer private yoga sessions for individuals or small groups. These sessions can be tailored to your specific needs and goals, whether you are a beginner or an experienced practitioner.
